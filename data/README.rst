====
Data
====

The data files come from the web site of the book:
https://sites.google.com/view/microeconometricswithr/table-of-contents

Files in the repository
-----------------------

* ``nls.csv`` (chapters 1, 2, 3, 6 and 8): NLSM data of Card (1995).
* ``hmda_aer.csv`` (chapter 2): Boston HMDA data.
* ``UpdatedStateLevelData-2010.dta`` (chapter 4): crime data by state, from the GitHub repository of the author of the book (https://github.com/christopherpadams/learningmicrometrics).
* ``NLSY97_min.csv`` and ``NLSY97_gender_book.csv`` (chapter 6): NLSY97 data.
* ``auctions.csv`` (chapter 9): timber auctions.
* ``cardkrueger.csv`` (chapters 10 and 12): Card and Krueger (1994).
* ``pubtwins.dta`` (chapter 12): twins data of Ashenfelter and Krueger (1994) and Ashenfelter and Rouse (1998). The book gives https://dataspace.princeton.edu/jspui/handle/88435/dsp01xg94hp567 as source. The file here comes from the web site of the book Mastering 'Metrics (https://www.masteringmetrics.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/pubtwins.dta).
* ``NLSY97Panel.csv`` and ``NLSY97Panel_names.csv`` (chapters 10 and 11): NLSY97 panel data and the names of its columns.
* ``bat_ave.csv`` (appendix A): batting averages.
